Avalon Game Making Tool Kit

Free tools for young game creators. Plan your game, build it, test it with players, and share it safely.

  • Free
  • No account
  • No network
  • Works offline

Every tool is one HTML file. Open it in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere, and it works without the internet.

Your work saves in this browser only. Browser saves can be erased or can differ when a file is opened from your computer, so click Save project file to keep a backup.

Plan and design

  • Game Balance & Economy Simulator Test rewards, prices, XP, drops, crafting and combat before players do.
  • Level & Quest Flow Designer Map levels and quests, then check that every path can be finished.
  • Game Feel Sandbox Play small demos, change one setting at a time, and save what feels right.
  • Multiplayer Session Planner Plan what every player does when they wait, play, join late, leave or come back.
  • Interface Accessibility Workshop Make game screens easy to read and use for everyone.

Build and ship

  • Roblox Game Creator Guide Plan your Roblox game step by step, track tasks and budget, and get ready to launch.
  • Asset Brief & Handoff Builder Write clear briefs for art, sound and models, and share them with your team.
  • Localization & Game Text Workspace Get your game text ready for players who speak other languages.
  • Playtest & Feedback Lab Plan a playtest, take notes while people play, and pick what to change next.

Grow and protect

  • Creator Marketing Toolkit Find resources, make a promo plan, create your materials, and track what happens.
  • Creator Rights Library Find free stories, music and art you can use, and check the rights before you do.
