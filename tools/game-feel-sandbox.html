Math and tips

These are simple math results and tips. They do not score your game. What you see testers do matters more.

Comfort & sound

You choose

Sound is off until you turn it on. A label on screen shows when a sound would play, even when muted.

Less motion turns off shake, sparkles, and menu movement. The player, the camera, and the hit signal still work. You can always pause.

New projects use the motion setting on your device. A project you open uses the motion setting it saved. Use the checkbox to change it.

Change A

A is your setup. Each change restarts A and B. Every number has a lowest and highest value. px means demo pixels. ms means milliseconds (1,000 ms = 1 second).

Before sharing

Your project file keeps data from every Avalon tool, even ones this tool cannot read. Check your notes before you share the file with anyone. Source links only open when you click them, and they need the internet.

Only a full project file (avalon.creator-project) can be opened here. The settings list and notes CSV are just for reading and sharing. You cannot open them back in.

How this tool works

Useful math

What it findsMathWhen it works
Time to top speedmaxSpeed / accelerationStarting still, holding one direction, on flat ground.
Stopping distancemaxSpeed² / (2 × deceleration)Moving at top speed, then letting go, on flat ground.
Best-case jump heightjumpImpulse² / (2 × gravity)A smooth math guess. The demo moves in tiny steps, so it is a little different.
Time to the top of a jumpjumpImpulse / gravityNothing in the way and no hit pause.
Camera catch-up1 - exp(-dt / followTime)Follow time is in seconds. Zero snaps right away. After one follow time, the camera closes about 63% of the gap.

All lowest and highest values, example setups, and tip triggers were picked for this tool. They are not limits from Roblox or any other engine.
